Chicken Alfredo

Chicken Alfredo is a creamy Italian-American pasta dish made with tender chicken, fettuccine, Parmesan cheese, and a rich Alfredo sauce.

Ingredients

  • 300 g fettuccine pasta
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • 100 g Parmesan cheese, grated
  • ½ teaspoon Italian herbs
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the fettuccine in salted water according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cut the chicken breasts into small pieces. Season with salt and pepper, then cook in olive oil until golden and fully cooked.
  3. CMelt the butter in the same pan. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Add the heavy cream and simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Add the cooked pasta and chicken to the sauce. Mix everything together until well coated..
  5. Add some extra Parmesan and black pepper, then serve immediately.
