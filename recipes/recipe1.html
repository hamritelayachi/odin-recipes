Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese, baked until golden and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 9–12 lasagna sheets
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 700 ml tomato sauce
  • 500 ml béchamel sauce
  • 200 g mozzarella cheese
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Prepare the meat sauce.
  2. Prepare the béchamel sauce.
  3. Cook the lasagna sheets.
  4. Layer the ingredients in a baking dish.
  5. Bake until golden and bubbling
