Pancakes

Pancakes are soft, fluffy, and golden breakfast treats made with flour, milk, eggs, and butter.

Ingredients

  • 200 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 250 ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract. Mix until just combined.
  3. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour some batter into the pan. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  5. Serve warm with your favorite toppings, such as syrup, fruit, or honey.
